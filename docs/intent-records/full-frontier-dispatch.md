title: Dispatch every useful independent task
date: 2026/10/09
source: @ottojung
kind: constraint

The orchestrator must delegate the whole independently useful frontier per pass, not just one task. It must recognize successful completed agent work despite open review issues, and delegate outstanding independent tasks instead of re-auditing old work. Only real work topology can prevent delegation; execution, capacity and tooling concerns are not scheduling criteria.
